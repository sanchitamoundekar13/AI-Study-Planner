# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Title

**AI Study Planner – Personalized and Intelligent Study Planning Assistant**

---

## 1. Product Overview

AI Study Planner is a web-based application designed to help students organize their study schedules according to their subjects, exam dates, topics, and available study time.

The product will initially provide a simple interface where students can enter their study information and view the submitted details.

The application will then be developed progressively to include Generative AI capabilities and, eventually, AI Agent and Agentic AI functionality.

The main purpose of the project is both to solve a practical student problem and to provide a practical learning path for understanding modern AI-powered application development.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Students often have difficulty creating and maintaining an effective study schedule.

Common problems include:

- Multiple subjects and topics
- Limited study time
- Different examination dates
- Difficulty prioritizing topics
- Lack of regular revision
- Difficulty adjusting a study plan when progress changes
- Time spent manually creating study schedules

A simple and intelligent study planning system can help students organize their available time more effectively.

---

## 3. Product Vision

The long-term vision is to create an intelligent study assistant that can understand a student's study goals, create personalized plans, use appropriate tools, monitor progress, and adapt the plan when circumstances change.

The product will evolve through the following stages:

```text
Basic Study Planner
        ↓
Generative AI Study Planner
        ↓
AI Study Planning Agent
        ↓
Agentic Study Assistant
```
